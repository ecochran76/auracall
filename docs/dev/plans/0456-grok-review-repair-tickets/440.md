## What to build

Resumed Grok history discovery and mirroring retain every row covered by a saved continuation, even when sidebar or open-chat rows overlap the bounded result.

Review priority: P1. Source: accepted review finding SPEC1, review commit a86dccd9516e8b974438419a1f9d96d1b394e5d6; implementation reviewed at dabde7cafe3ae9b0c64757b9a0f27c3e96a56a10.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A public discovery regression reproduces sidebar rows a/b crowding resumed history c/d out of a two-row result while its cursor advances after d; the repair prevents that omission.
- [ ] Returned bounds, filtering, supplemental rows and the emitted continuation describe one consistent qualified row set; continuation never skips rows omitted by the public result.
- [ ] The real collector durably commits those rows and continuation together; restart, cancellation and yield neither lose rows nor advance an uncommitted cursor.
- [ ] Controlled-CDP and isolated-store tests exercise the public discovery-to-mirror path; affected CLI/API/MCP coverage and Ops status remain consistent without exposing private cursor data.
