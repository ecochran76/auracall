## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An unchanged completed account larger than one cycle remains in follow mode rather than resetting every scope and replaying the archive.
- [ ] New or changed conversations and new project scopes are discovered without discarding completed scope state; the chosen incremental frontier is justified by the provider ordering/evidence it uses.
- [ ] Cancellation, restart and foreground yield preserve account-scoped incremental state; stale cached success never masks a failed fresh observation.
- [ ] Explicit full sweeps remain available and bounded, with partial coverage distinguished from confirmed observed completion.
- [ ] Public collector/service tests prove unchanged, changed, new-scope and interrupted cycles and consistent phase/coverage readback across CLI/API/MCP/Ops.
